# TableFlow Reservations Guide

## Booking widget
Add the booking widget to your website by pasting one line of JavaScript. Guests can book without creating an account.
By default guests can book up to 60 days in advance. You can raise this limit up to 365 days.

## Party size
Each table has a maximum party size. Parties of more than 10 guests require manual approval by default.
Up to 4 tables can be combined to seat a larger party.

## Deposits
Deposits are available on the Growth and Pro plans only. The deposit is set per guest, between $5 and $100, and defaults to $10.
Deposits can be required automatically for parties of 6 or more.
If a guest cancels at least 24 hours before the reservation, the deposit is refunded automatically within 5 to 7 business days.
If the guest cancels later than that, the restaurant keeps the deposit.

## No-show fees
A restaurant can charge a no-show fee of up to $25 per guest. The guest must agree to the fee when booking.

## Cancellation window
The default cancellation window is 24 hours. You can configure it anywhere from 1 to 72 hours.

## Waitlist
The walk-in waitlist sends an SMS to the guest when their table is ready. Guests have 10 minutes to respond by default before they are removed from the list.
You can change the response time to anything between 5 and 20 minutes.

## Overbooking protection
Pacing limits stop too many guests arriving at once. The default is 20 covers per 15 minutes.
