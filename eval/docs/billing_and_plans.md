# TableFlow Billing and Plans

## Plans
Starter costs $49 per location per month and includes up to 5 staff accounts, online reservations and basic reports.
Growth costs $129 per location per month and includes up to 25 staff accounts, deposits and prepayments, POS integrations and 500 SMS per month.
Pro costs $299 per location per month and includes unlimited staff accounts, API access, custom roles, priority support and 2,000 SMS per month.
SMS messages beyond the included amount are billed at $0.04 per SMS.

## Free trial
Every new account gets a 14-day free trial with Growth features. No credit card is required to start the trial.
If you do not choose a plan when the trial ends, the account becomes read-only and your data is kept for 60 days.

## Annual billing
Paying yearly instead of monthly saves 20 percent.
Bank transfer (ACH) is accepted only for annual Pro plans. All other plans pay by Visa, Mastercard or American Express.

## Invoices
You are charged on the same day of the month that you signed up. Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and can be downloaded from Settings > Billing.

## Failed payments
A failed payment is retried 3 times over 7 days, on day 1, day 3 and day 7. After the final failed attempt the account is suspended. It is reactivated immediately once payment succeeds.

## Refunds
Monthly subscriptions are not refundable, except for a full refund requested within the first 7 days of your first paid month.
Annual subscriptions get a pro-rated refund if cancelled within the first 30 days.

## Changing or cancelling a plan
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date.
You can cancel at any time from Settings > Billing, and access continues until the end of the paid period.
