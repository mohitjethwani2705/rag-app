TableFlow Staff and Permissions

ROLES
Owner has full access including billing and can delete the account.
Manager has access to everything except billing and deleting the account.
Host can manage reservations and the waitlist and can view guest profiles, but cannot see reports.
Server can only view reservations for their own section.
Custom roles are available on the Pro plan only.

INVITING STAFF
Invite staff from Settings > Team > Invite. Invitation links expire after 72 hours.
The number of staff accounts you can have depends on your plan.
Deactivating a staff member revokes their access immediately. Their history is kept.

SECURITY
Two-factor authentication works with authenticator apps. SMS codes are not supported.
Two-factor authentication is mandatory for Owners and Managers on the Pro plan and optional on other plans.
Password reset links are valid for 30 minutes.
After 5 failed login attempts an account is locked for 15 minutes.
Web sessions time out after 8 hours of inactivity.

AUDIT LOG
The audit log shows who changed reservations and settings. It is available on the Pro plan only and entries are kept for 12 months.
