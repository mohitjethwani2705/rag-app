# TableFlow Data, Privacy and Support

## Hosting and encryption
Data is hosted on AWS. US-East is the default region, and EU customers can choose EU-Frankfurt.
Data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2 or higher in transit.
Card details are never stored by TableFlow. Payments are handled by Stripe, which is PCI DSS compliant.

## Backups
Backups run daily and are kept for 35 days. Point-in-time recovery is available for the last 7 days.

## Retention and privacy
Guest data is kept while your account is active and for 90 days after cancellation, then permanently deleted.
Guest data export (CSV) and deletion requests are honored within 30 days. Submit them from Settings > Privacy > Guest Requests.

## Compliance
TableFlow is SOC 2 Type II certified. The report is available under NDA.

## Uptime
Pro plans have a 99.9 percent uptime SLA. Starter and Growth target 99.5 percent on a best-effort basis.

## Support
Starter has email support only, with a response within 2 business days.
Growth has email and chat support from 9am to 6pm Eastern, Monday to Friday, with a response within 8 hours.
Pro has priority phone and chat support 24/7, with a response within 1 hour.
